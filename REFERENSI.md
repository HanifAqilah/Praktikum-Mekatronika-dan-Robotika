# Referensi Buku & Dokumentasi
**Praktikum Mekatronika dan Robotika**

---

## 📖 Mekatronika (Mekanik + Elektronik + Sistem)

### Buku Utama
1. **W. Bolton — Mechatronics: Electronic Control Systems in Mechanical and Electrical Engineering (7th ed.)**
   - Publisher: Pearson
   - **Coverage:** Sensor, aktuator, controller, interfacing
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 1-5, 7
   - **Level:** Intermediate
   - **Rekomendasi:** Beli atau akses perpustakaan

2. **David G. Alciatore, Michael B. Histand — Introduction to Mechatronics and Measurement Systems (5th ed.)**
   - Publisher: McGraw-Hill
   - **Coverage:** Sistem pengukuran, sensor conditioning, interfacing
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 3-4
   - **Level:** Beginner-Intermediate
   - **Note:** Good untuk fundamental measurement & signal conditioning

3. **Devdas Shetty, Richard A. Kolk — Mechatronics System Design (2nd ed.)**
   - Publisher: Cengage Learning
   - **Coverage:** Desain sistem mekatronika dari konsep sampai implementasi
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 1-2, 7
   - **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
   - **Rekomendasi:** Referensi untuk design methodology

## 🤖 Robotika (Kinematika, Perencanaan, Kontrol)

### Buku Utama (WAJIB)
1. **Kevin M. Lynch & Frank C. Park — Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control**
   - Publisher: Cambridge University Press
   - **Coverage:** Kinematika, dinamika, trajectory planning, control
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 8-9, 12
   - **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
   - **⭐ Rating:** 5/5 (industri standard)
   - **Free Resources:** http://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/index.php/Modern_Robotics
   - **Note:** Ada video lecture gratis online

2. **John J. Craig — Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control (4th ed.)**
   - Publisher: Pearson
   - **Coverage:** Kinematics, dynamics, trajectory, control
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 8-9
   - **Level:** Beginner-Intermediate
   - **Note:** Lebih mudah dipahami dari Lynch & Park, cocok untuk pemula

3. **Bruno Siciliano, Lorenzo Sciavicco, Luigi Villani, Giuseppe Oriolo — Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control**
   - Publisher: Springer
   - **Coverage:** Kinematics, dynamics, planning, control (comprehensive)
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 8-9, 12
   - **Level:** Advanced
   - **Note:** Referensi akademis yang mendalam

## 🐧 ROS2 (Ubuntu Robot Software Stack)

### Official Documentation
- **ROS 2 Official Documentation:** https://docs.ros.org/en/humble/
- **ROS 2 Humble (LTS):** Dokumentasi lengkap, tutorial, API reference

### Buku Rekomendasi
1. **ROS 2 from Scratch**
   - Publisher: Packt
   - **Coverage:** ROS2 fundamental, node, topic, service, action
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 10
   - **Level:** Beginner
   - **Note:** Tutorial praktis untuk pemula ROS2

2. **Mastering ROS 2 for Robotics Programming (4th ed.)**
   - Publisher: Packt
   - **Coverage:** Advanced ROS2: navigation, manipulation, perception, integration
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 10-13
   - **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
   - **Rekomendasi:** Bagus untuk project integration

3. **A Concise Introduction to Robot Programming with ROS2**
   - Publisher: CRC Press
   - **Coverage:** ROS2 basics dengan fokus hands-on project
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 10-12
   - **Level:** Beginner-Intermediate
   - **Note:** Cocok untuk praktikum

## 🎛️ Sistem Kontrol

### Buku Utama
1. **Norman S. Nise — Control Systems Engineering (8th ed.)**
   - Publisher: Wiley
   - **Coverage:** Teori kontrol klasik, transfer function, PID, root locus, Bode plot
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 6, 12
   - **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
   - **Note:** Standar akademis untuk kontrol sistem

2. **Karl J. Åström, Richard M. Murray — Feedback Systems: An Introduction for Scientists and Engineers**
   - Publisher: Princeton University Press
   - **Coverage:** Feedback control modern, design methodology
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 6, 12
   - **Level:** Intermediate
   - **Free:** https://fbswiki.org/
   - **Note:** Pendekatan modern & practical

### Paper Klasik
3. **Kiam Heong Ang, G. Chong, Yun Li — "PID Control System Analysis, Design, and Technology"**
   - **Journal:** IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology, 2005
   - **Coverage:** PID controller: tuning, implementation, applications
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 6
   - **Level:** Advanced
   - **Type:** Journal paper (academic)

## 🏭 PLC, SCADA, Protokol Industri

### PLC Programming
1. **Frank D. Petruzella — Programmable Logic Controllers (6th ed.)**
   - Publisher: McGraw-Hill
   - **Coverage:** PLC programming, ladder logic, industrial applications
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 2, 12 (optional)
   - **Level:** Beginner-Intermediate
   - **Note:** Standar untuk belajar PLC

### SCADA & Industrial Communication
2. **David Bailey & Edwin Wright — Practical SCADA for Industry**
   - Publisher: Newnes/Elsevier
   - **Coverage:** SCADA implementation, monitoring, alarming
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 13-14
   - **Level:** Intermediate
   - **Note:** Implementasi praktis

3. **Gordon R. Clarke, Deon Reynders, Edwin Wright — Practical Modern SCADA Protocols: DNP3, 60870.5 and Related Systems**
   - Publisher: Newnes/Elsevier
   - **Coverage:** Protokol komunikasi SCADA (DNP3, 60870.5, IEC 61850)
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 11-13 (advanced optional)
   - **Level:** Advanced
   - **Note:** Industry standard protocols

4. **Wolfgang Mahnke, Stefan-Helmut Leitner, Matthias Damm — OPC Unified Architecture**
   - Publisher: Springer
   - **Coverage:** OPC UA untuk Industry 4.0
   - **Relevant untuk:** Modul 13-14 (advanced optional)
   - **Level:** Advanced
   - **Note:** Untuk integration dengan SCADA/MES

## 🔗 Citation & Bibliography

Ketika menggunakan referensi, gunakan format IEEE atau Harvard:

### IEEE Format
```
[1] W. Bolton, Mechatronics: Electronic Control Systems in Mechanical 
    and Electrical Engineering, 7th ed. Pearson, 2019.

[2] K. M. Lynch and F. C. Park, Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, 
    and Control. Cambridge University Press, 2017.
```

### Harvard Format
```
Bolton, W. (2019) Mechatronics: Electronic Control Systems in Mechanical 
and Electrical Engineering. 7th ed. Pearson.

Lynch, K.M. and Park, F.C. (2017) Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, 
and Control. Cambridge University Press.
```
